House rules

The promises every Orange Mine game keeps.

  • Every level can be finishedEach level is checked by a solver before you ever see it. No impossible boards, no luck needed.
  • Nothing is riggedWhat comes next never changes because you are close to losing, or to push you towards an ad.
  • Ads stay politeNever in the middle of a level, at most one full-screen ad every two minutes, and videos with rewards are always your choice.
  • Your progress stays with youNo accounts and no sign-in. Levels, coins and friends are saved on your phone.
