Privacy policy for Shelf Stack, Send Home, Sort Office, Lost & Found, Lost Bin, Out the Door and Quiet Rooms

Last updated: 2026-11-15

These games are made by Orange Mine ("we"). This policy explains what information the games use and why.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The games have no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your scores, levels, coins, helpers, missions and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The games are free and show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
- information about how you interact with ads and the app,
- crash and performance information about ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Ad privacy, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Rewarded videos

Some features (for example a second chance, doubled coins or a hint) are offered in exchange for watching a video ad. Watching is always optional.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob described above, no data leaves your device.

Security

Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the text on this page.
